# Media pitch: [newsworthy development]

## Subject options

- [Concrete development] and why it matters for [relevant wider issue]
- New evidence from Koinos on [specific technical or user problem]

## Draft

Hello [name],

[One sentence showing why this development fits the journalist’s audience or recent coverage.]

[Two or three sentences stating what happened, when it happened, and why it matters beyond the Koinos community. Avoid slogans and price framing.]

Primary evidence:

- [release, proposal, repository, transaction, dataset, or documentation]
- [second independent or primary source]

[Named accountable source] can discuss [specific area], and [named technical source] can explain [specific mechanism or limitation].

This material is [first-party/community/independent], and [state any sponsorship or material relationship].

Would this be relevant to your coverage of [specific topic]?

[Public contact or authorized signature]

## Pre-send checks

- [ ] The development is current and newsworthy.
- [ ] The recipient and their recent coverage were verified.
- [ ] Every claim is supported by a link included or available on request.
- [ ] Spokespeople agreed to be named and are available.
- [ ] Financial and organizational relationships are disclosed.
- [ ] The exact recipient and final draft are approved before sending.
