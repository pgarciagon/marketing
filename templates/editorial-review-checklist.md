# Editorial review checklist

Use this checklist in the pull request or copy it into the content document.

## Purpose and audience

- [ ] One primary audience is named.
- [ ] The reader problem and call to action are clear.
- [ ] The content is appropriate for the chosen channel.

## Evidence

- [ ] Material factual claims have sources.
- [ ] Time-sensitive claims were checked on a stated date.
- [ ] Proposals, prototypes, testnet activity, releases, and live services are distinguished.
- [ ] Inferences are labeled rather than presented as direct facts.
- [ ] Quotes and contributor attributions match the source.

## Koinos terminology

- [ ] Koinos, KOIN, VHP, and wrapped KOIN representations are not conflated.
- [ ] Mana and “feeless” are explained accurately for the intended audience.
- [ ] Governance and decentralization claims match current evidence.
- [ ] Comparative or “first” claims have a current methodology or were removed.

## Editorial and brand quality

- [ ] The message is consistent with the [message house](../brand/message-house.md).
- [ ] The piece uses plain language and defines necessary specialist terms.
- [ ] The title and opening describe the actual subject without clickbait.
- [ ] Official logos and colors follow the current media kit.
- [ ] Images have sources, permission, captions when needed, and useful alt text.
- [ ] Sponsorships and material relationships are disclosed.

## Safety and publication

- [ ] No credentials, private correspondence, or unpublished personal information are included.
- [ ] No investment promises or misleading market claims are present.
- [ ] The exact destination and publication authority are confirmed.
- [ ] Final links, images, formatting, and metadata will be checked after publication.
- [ ] A public URL and results owner will be recorded.
