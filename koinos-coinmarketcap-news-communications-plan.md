# Koinos CoinMarketCap News Communications Plan

- **Prepared:** September 13, 2026
- **Scope:** Increase the probability that relevant Koinos coverage appears automatically in the **Koinos News** module on CoinMarketCap and improve Koinos visibility across CoinMarketCap Community.
- **Asset page:** <https://coinmarketcap.com/currencies/koinos/>
- **CoinMarketCap UCID:** `8282`

## 1. Executive summary

The Koinos asset page currently displays an empty **Koinos News** module even though the official Koinos Network publication has recent articles and a functioning RSS feed. This indicates that publishing on Medium alone is not sufficient. CoinMarketCap controls which publishers enter its Headlines system, how articles are matched to an asset, and how content is ranked.

Koinos cannot guarantee placement, but it can influence every controllable input through four coordinated workstreams:

1. Establish an official, verified Koinos presence on CoinMarketCap Community.
2. Ensure CoinMarketCap associates the official Koinos publication and RSS feed with the KOIN asset record.
3. Request direct onboarding of the publication into CoinMarketCap Headlines.
4. Earn independent coverage from publishers that CoinMarketCap already recognizes.

The communications objective is not to manufacture activity or repeatedly ask CMC for placement. It is to make Koinos easy to identify, verify, ingest, classify, and cover.

## 2. Current-state assessment

### Confirmed observations

- The live [Koinos CoinMarketCap page](https://coinmarketcap.com/currencies/koinos/) displays the **Koinos News** section but no articles.
- The [Koinos Network Medium publication](https://medium.com/koinosnetwork) is active.
- Its [RSS 2.0 feed](https://medium.com/feed/koinosnetwork) responded successfully when checked on September 13, 2026 and contained recent Koinos articles.
- The Koinos CoinMarketCap record does not visibly present the Medium publication as a Blog or Message Board link.
- CoinMarketCap documents a process for adding a publication to **CoinMarketCap Headlines**, but the linked onboarding URL currently opens the general request selector and does not expose a dedicated Headlines option.
- CoinMarketCap separately offers project verification and publishing on **CMC Community**. Community content can improve visibility, but it is not the same product as the News module.

### Working conclusion

The most likely missing connection is not article supply. It is source approval and/or entity association between the official publication, the RSS feed, and Koinos/KOIN/UCID 8282.

CoinMarketCap does not publicly document the complete matching or ranking algorithm. Any claim about its internal weighting must therefore be treated as an inference. The plan below focuses on documented CMC submission routes and observable publishing behavior.

## 5. Message framework

Every communication should reinforce four points:

1. **Identity:** Koinos is the asset identified as KOIN and UCID 8282 on CoinMarketCap.
2. **Verifiability:** Claims link to releases, repositories, governance records, applications, network data, or named sources.
3. **Relevance:** Each article explains why the development matters beyond a promotional announcement.
4. **Editorial independence:** Koinos is asking CMC and journalists to evaluate accurate sources, not to guarantee favorable coverage.

Avoid unsupported superlatives, price predictions, ticker stuffing, vague partnership announcements, or language that implies CMC endorsement.

## 9. Workstream D: Independent editorial coverage

If the News module primarily draws from approved publishers, independent reporting is likely to be a stronger input than additional first-party announcements. This is an inference, not a published CMC guarantee.

### What to pitch

- A materially important protocol release or security/correctness improvement.
- A governance decision with a clear consequence for users or builders.
- A measurable application, developer, or operator adoption result.
- An original technical finding with relevance to the broader blockchain industry.
- A significant launch that readers can verify and use.
- A transparent postmortem with technical evidence and lessons for other networks.

### Press package for each pitch

- A one-paragraph news summary.
- Why it matters outside the Koinos community.
- Primary links: release, code, proposal, transaction, documentation, or dataset.
- One accountable spokesperson and one technical source.
- A media-kit link with approved visual assets.
- Disclosure of any financial, organizational, or contributor relationship.

Send narrow, personalized pitches to relevant editors. Do not purchase undisclosed coverage or ask several community members to send identical messages.

## 10. Publishing and technical standards

For new articles:

- Identify the subject as **Koinos (KOIN)** naturally near the beginning.
- Use a descriptive headline tied to the actual event.
- Include a clear publication date and author.
- Link to primary evidence and the canonical Koinos resource.
- Add an appropriate canonical URL where the platform permits it.
- Provide an informative summary and lead image.
- Keep RSS entries complete and stable after publication.
- Use consistent project naming; avoid ambiguous references to `KOIN` without `Koinos`.
- Correct factual errors transparently rather than silently rewriting material claims.

Longer term, Koinos should consider a first-party newsroom on `koinos.io` with its own RSS or Atom feed. Medium can remain a distribution channel, but a first-party canonical source provides stronger identity, durable URLs, and direct control over structured metadata.

- CMC asset metadata updated: yes/no.
- Official Community account verified: yes/no.
- Headlines onboarding response received: yes/no and outcome.
- Number of eligible articles appearing in Koinos News.
- Delay between article publication and CMC appearance.
- Publisher and article type for each successful inclusion.
- CMC Community reach, meaningful comments, and follows.
- Referral visits from CoinMarketCap to official Koinos resources.

The primary success criterion is at least one accurately attributed article appearing automatically in the Koinos News module, followed by repeatable inclusion from a stable source.

## 13. Risks and safeguards

- **No placement guarantee:** CMC retains editorial and algorithmic control.
- **Stale support documentation:** Ask CMC to confirm the current Headlines path before assuming the old form remains operational.
- **Duplicate submissions:** Consolidate evidence and use one responsible submitter.
- **Identity ambiguity:** Use an authorized representative and official-domain email.
- **Community spam:** Never coordinate repetitive ticker posts, synthetic engagement, or mass support requests.
- **Editorial credibility:** Separate first-party announcements from independent reporting.
- **Overclaiming:** Support all adoption, security, partnership, and performance claims with current evidence.
- **Publication authority:** Draft external messages first and obtain the appropriate approval before submitting, posting, or sending them.

## 14. Decision point after 30 days

If CMC confirms that the official Medium/RSS source is eligible, complete its onboarding requirements and test the result with the next substantive article.

If CMC declines or no longer accepts project publications into Headlines, prioritize verified CMC Community publishing, first-party newsroom infrastructure, and independent coverage from existing CMC-recognized publishers. Do not compensate for rejection with repeated forms or coordinated pressure.
