## Evidence labels

Use these labels in research notes when useful:

- **Confirmed current:** verified against a live authoritative source on a stated date.
- **Confirmed historical:** supported by a stable primary or archived source.
- **Direct account:** a person’s attributable statement about their own role or experience.
- **Independent report:** published by a source outside the project.
- **Inference:** a conclusion drawn from evidence but not stated directly by the source.
- **Unresolved:** conflicting or insufficient evidence.
- **Proposal:** intended future work, not current state.

## Research rules

- Preserve source URLs, dates, and the relevant claim.
- Prefer primary sources for releases, code, contracts, governance, and official roles.
- Recheck time-sensitive facts immediately before publication.
- Do not infer identity, ownership, authorship, or organizational authority from activity alone.
- Do not turn private chat content into public evidence without specific authorization.
- Keep quantitative contribution data separate from qualitative judgments.
- Record contradictory evidence rather than hiding it.

When a research conclusion changes the documentary master, follow the synchronization requirements in `AGENTS.md`.
